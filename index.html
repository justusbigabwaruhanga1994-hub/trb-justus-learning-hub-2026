How the Digital Library Works

Keep your original files in Google Drive and share a link to each resource.

1. Upload to Google Drive

Upload your PDF, document or other learning material to your Google Drive. Use clear file names, for example “Primary 4 Mathematics – Fractions”.

2. Allow free viewing

In Drive, open Share settings and set General access to Anyone with the link — Viewer. This allows people with the link to open it without requesting access, subject to Google/account restrictions.

3. Add it to the website

Add the title, level, subject and Google Drive share link to the resource list in the website file. Then publish the updated file.

4. Make a QR code

Paste the same share link into the QR Code Maker below and generate a QR image. You can print it on the book, worksheet or classroom notice.
