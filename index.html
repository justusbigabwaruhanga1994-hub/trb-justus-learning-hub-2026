About Us

TR B. JUSTUS EDUCATION HUB is an educational resource platform created to connect people with learning materials and encourage the pursuit of knowledge.

Our Vision

To contribute to a society in which learners and educators can access useful educational resources and opportunities for lifelong learning.

Our Mission

To make selected educational materials accessible and to support teaching, learning, revision and the sharing of knowledge.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide a useful, accessible and welcoming platform for learners, teachers, parents and education lovers.

Our Core Belief

Education and knowledge can open doors to personal growth and wider opportunities.

Our Objectives

  1. To promote access to educational books and learning resources.
  2. To support teachers and learners in teaching, revision and independent study.
  3. To encourage a culture of reading, learning and lifelong education.
  4. To provide a convenient online place for selected educational materials.
  5. To encourage the sharing of useful knowledge among education lovers.
