About TR B. JUSTUS EDUCATION HUB

Who We Are

TR B. JUSTUS EDUCATION HUB is an educational resource platform established to extend knowledge and make quality learning materials accessible to learners, teachers and other people who value education.

We recognise that education opens doors to opportunity. Our aim is to support learning by connecting people with useful books, educational information and learning resources.

Our Vision

To become a trusted and accessible educational resource centre that promotes knowledge, learning and educational opportunity for everyone.

Our Mission

To provide and share quality educational resources that help learners and teachers develop knowledge, skills and confidence, regardless of their social or economic background.

Our Commitment

We are committed to making learning materials available to people at different levels of education. We believe that a learner's opportunity to gain knowledge should not be limited simply because of the income of their family.

Through this platform, we seek to encourage reading, independent study, continuous learning and the responsible use of educational materials.

Our Core Belief

Knowledge is a powerful tool for personal growth, community development and a better future. Every learner deserves an opportunity to learn and improve.

Our Objectives

TR B. JUSTUS EDUCATION HUB aims to:

  1. Provide and share quality educational books and learning materials.
  2. Support learners at different levels of education with useful learning resources.
  3. Assist teachers and educators in accessing materials that support teaching and learning.
  4. Encourage reading, independent study, critical thinking and lifelong learning.
  5. Promote fairer access to educational resources, including for learners from low-income households.
  6. Help people develop knowledge, skills, confidence and a positive attitude towards education.
  7. Encourage the responsible sharing and use of educational materials.
Our Promise: To keep promoting education, sharing knowledge and supporting the learning journey of every person we can reach.
